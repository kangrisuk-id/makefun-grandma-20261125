## Criteria score of the shortlist, first difference first

1. **Reads as the single word a grandma would text about AI** — `computo` reads as her typing of
   computer, i.e. the machine behind the AI; `robotty` reads as robot; `puter` as her word for
   computer; `magicbox` and `chatterbox` as the box. All five pass criterion 1.
2. **Warm and a little baffled** — `computo` (she heard "computer" her whole life and types it
   the way she says it), `robotty` (typo voice), `puter` (her word), `magicbox` (a bit baffled,
   the box works like magic), `chatterbox`.
3. **Funny in one word, joke lands without a caption** — `robotty` and `magicbox` are the funniest;
   `computo` is warm rather than punchy.
4. **Tied to AI/robots/machines, not any subject** — `computo` (computer), `robotty` (robot),
   `puter` (computer), `magicbox`/`chatterbox` (boxes that work/talk).
5. **Fresh, not the obvious pick** — `computo` is not one of the four seeds and not a commonly
   repeated choice; `robotty` is fresh too, but reads more like the seed `robit` with a tweak.

First criterion where the shortlist differs: criterion 2 (warm voice). Among the near-tie rows
(`computo` vs `puter`), criterion 3 (funny) gives the win to the rest; between `computo` and the
rest of the table the tie falls to criterion 5 on freshness.

## Why computo won

- **About AI, unmistakably**: it is the computer — the machine the AI runs on. Not vague, not
  human, not a phrase: `computo` is one word that says machine.
- **Unmistakably a grandmother's typing**: the typo lane. She has heard the word "computer" for
  years and, like her saying "artificial" would come out as `artifishul`, saying "computer"
  comes out as `computo`. Warm and a little baffled, the way she would text her family.
- **Funny in one word without a caption**: the joke is her casually calling the thing that
  talks to her the computo; it reads as a real grandparents' text.
- **Fresh, not the obvious**: not `Skynet`/`Alexa`/`Robot`/`AI`/`ChatGPT`, and not a copy of any
  seed — `computo` is its own pick.
- **Readable everywhere**: 7 lowercase letters, 8-character gate pass, no spaces, no newline.

Word: **computo** — length 7 characters.
